        There are two main routes for students to work with our [research group](/esgi/). Before reaching out, take a look at our [recent publications](/#papers) and [current PhD students](/esgi/) to see how your interests align with our work.

        <details>
        <summary><strong>🎓 PhD Positions</strong></summary>

        I supervise PhD students through the School of Informatics' postgraduate research programme. Prospective applicants should apply under one of the [available Centres for Doctoral Training (CDTs) and scholarships](https://informatics.ed.ac.uk/study-with-us/our-degrees/postgraduate-research-and-cdts/centres-doctoral-training-cdts).

        **When emailing me about a PhD, please include:**
        - Your CV
        - A brief statement of your research interests and motivation, ideally linked to our group's research themes
        - Your intended start date
        - The scholarship/CDT you plan to apply through, if known

        </details>

        <details>
        <summary><strong>✈️ Visiting Periods</strong></summary>

        I welcome visiting students who wish to spend a period of time working with the group. Please keep the following in mind when planning a visit:

        - **ATAS-required visits**: if you require an [ATAS (Academic Technology Approval Scheme)](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/academic-technology-approval-scheme) certificate, the University's International Graduate School (IGS) needs to receive your request **at least five months** ahead of the start of your planned visit.
        - **Non-ATAS international visits**: if you are an international visiting student who does not require ATAS, IGS needs to receive your request **at least two months** ahead of the start of your planned visit.
        - **One visit per year**: Visiting Research students are permitted to register **only once in any 12-month rolling period**. Multiple split visits (e.g. 3 months on programme, 1 month break, 3 months on programme) are **not permitted**.

        Given these timelines, please get in touch as early as possible so there's enough time to process the request.

        **When emailing me about a visiting period, please include:**
        - Your CV
        - A brief statement of your research interests and motivation for visiting
        - Your proposed visit dates
        - Whether you require an ATAS certificate

        </details>

        These collaborations are most commonly arranged through the University of Edinburgh's [Edinburgh Innovations](https://edinburgh-innovations.ed.ac.uk/), which supports formal industry partnerships, consultancy and collaborative research agreements.

        A few other options for collaboration are also possible, including:
        - **Funded research projects**: collaborative research addressing challenges relevant to your organisation
        - **Sponsored PhD scholarships**: co-funding a PhD student to work on a shared research agenda
        - **Focused long-term collaborations**: sustained partnerships built around a specific research direction

        Other arrangements are also possible depending on the nature and scope of the collaboration.
